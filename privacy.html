Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

MiDinero Web (artlp.github.io/midinero-web, formerly Bluecoins Web) is a web page for viewing and editing personal finance data from the MiDinero and Bluecoins Android apps. It is an independent tool and is not made by or affiliated with the developers of Bluecoins.

What data the app accesses

When you choose to connect Google Drive, the app asks for the drive.file permission. With it, the app can only access:

  • the files you select yourself in the Google file picker: the Bluecoins sync files bluecoins.fydb and tracker_file;
  • backup copies of bluecoins.fydb that the app creates in your Drive before it saves changes.

It cannot see or change any other file in your Google Drive. You can also use the app without Google Drive by opening a file from your device.

When you choose to connect Dropbox instead, the app asks for access to your Dropbox files. It uses it only to find the folder where Bluecoins keeps tracker_file and its .fydb file (or the MiDinero folder where MiDinero keeps them), read and update those two files, and save a backup copy of the .fydb file in a Bluecoins Web backups folder before each upload. You can remove the app's access at any time in Dropbox under Settings › Connected apps.

How the data is used

The files are downloaded into your browser, where the app shows your accounts, transactions and reports. When you add, edit or delete transactions and choose to upload, the app writes the updated files back to the same files in your Drive so the phone app can sync them. The data is used only to provide these features to you.

Where the data is stored

MiDinero Web has no server and no database of its own. Your financial data never leaves your browser except when it is uploaded back to your own Google Drive or Dropbox. In your browser the app keeps:

  • the last file you opened from your device, so it opens again next time (remove it with Forget file or by clearing site data);
  • the temporary Google sign-in token, which expires within an hour, and your Google account's email address, used only to tell Google which account to sign you back in with;
  • if you connect Dropbox, the Dropbox sign-in tokens (one of them lasts until you remove the app's access, so you stay signed in on that browser) and the path of the Bluecoins folder in your Dropbox and whether you last opened Bluecoins or MiDinero data.

Exchange rates

To fill in today's exchange rate for new transactions, the app requests public rates from ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com). The request contains only your main currency code (for example EUR), never your transactions or any personal data.

Sharing

The app does not share, sell or transfer your data to anyone. It has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not use your data to train any AI or machine-learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

MiDinero Web's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can remove the app's access to your Google Drive at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Backup copies the app created stay in your Drive until you delete them.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/artlp/midinero-web/issues.
