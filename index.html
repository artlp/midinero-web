MiDinero Web

Web version of your MiDinero and Bluecoins data

Open your finance data

MiDinero Web lets you view and edit the finances you keep in the MiDinero or Bluecoins Android app from any browser: account balances, transactions, transfers and monthly reports. It opens the sync files those apps keep in your Google Drive or Dropbox, and saves your edits back to them so the phone app picks them up. Everything is processed in this browser; there is no server.

With Google Drive, the app only gets access to the two files you pick (bluecoins.fydb and tracker_file), never the rest of your Drive. With Dropbox, it finds the folder holding tracker_file by itself and stays signed in on this browser; data that MiDinero syncs to Dropbox (folder /MiDinero) opens the same way. You can also open a downloaded .fydb file instead.
